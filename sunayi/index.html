Your dashboard includes
  • Recording and transcript of every call
  • Topic, outcome and confidence per call
  • Follow-ups and escalations flagged
  • Minutes used, calls per week and month, average call length
  • What callers ask about, calls by hour and day
  • Contact export to CSV
