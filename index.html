About Us

Built for Businesses That Live on the Phone

Varcera builds AI voice agents and automation systems for restaurants and healthcare providers first, with more industries on the way. We started because we watched local restaurants lose real customers to a phone that just rang and rang. Our AI answers instead — accurately, politely, and every single time.

What we build isn't a chatbot bolted onto a website. It's a voice agent that picks up the phone, holds an actual conversation, takes the reservation or books the appointment, and hands off to a real person the moment it's needed. Every agent is built around your real hours, menu or services, and staff — and every conversation is logged so nothing falls through the cracks.
