Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

What we collect

When you send us a message through the contact form on varcera.in, or ask Varcera for a demo through an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, we receive what you submit: your name, your email address, your message, and for assistant requests the business name if you give one. We also note the time and where the request came from (the website or an assistant).

We do not collect anything else from you through the website or the assistant connection, and we do not ask for payment details or passwords.

How we use it

  • To reply to you about Varcera and Sunayi.
  • To arrange a demo if you asked for one.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Messages are stored in our own business systems, hosted with cloud providers, and a copy is emailed to us by our form provider. Only the Varcera team can read them.

Phone calls to Sunayi

Calls to a restaurant that uses Sunayi are handled for that restaurant, which controls its own call recordings and transcripts. The demo recordings on varcera.in/sunayi are calls placed to Sunayi for demonstration.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, email varcera.corp@gmail.com and we will do it.
